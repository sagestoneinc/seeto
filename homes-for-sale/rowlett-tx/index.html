Common questions about Rowlett

What school district serves Rowlett, TX?

Rowlett is served by Garland ISD and Rockwall ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is Rowlett, TX in?

Rowlett is located in Dallas and Rockwall Counties, in the Dallas-Fort Worth area of Texas.
