Common questions about Spring

What school district serves Spring, TX?

Spring is served by Spring ISD and Klein ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is Spring, TX in?

Spring is located in Harris County, in the Houston area of Texas.

Does Seeto Realty work with buyers and sellers in Spring?

Yes. Seeto Realty represents both buyers and sellers in Spring and across the Houston area. The brokerage is based in Plano and has operated in Texas real estate since 2010.
