Common questions about Prosper

What school district serves Prosper, TX?

Prosper is served by Prosper ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is Prosper, TX in?

Prosper is located in Collin and Denton Counties, in the Dallas-Fort Worth area of Texas.
