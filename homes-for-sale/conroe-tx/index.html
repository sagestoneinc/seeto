Common questions about Conroe

What school district serves Conroe, TX?

Conroe is served by Conroe ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is Conroe, TX in?

Conroe is located in Montgomery County, in the Houston area of Texas.

Does Seeto Realty work with buyers and sellers in Conroe?

Yes. Seeto Realty represents both buyers and sellers in Conroe and across the Houston area. The brokerage is based in Plano and has operated in Texas real estate since 2010.
