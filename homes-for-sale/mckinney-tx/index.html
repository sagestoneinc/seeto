Common questions about McKinney

What school district serves McKinney, TX?

McKinney is served by McKinney ISD, Prosper ISD, and Frisco ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is McKinney, TX in?

McKinney is located in Collin County, in the Dallas-Fort Worth area of Texas.

Does Seeto Realty work with buyers and sellers in McKinney?

Yes. Seeto Realty represents both buyers and sellers in McKinney and across the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The brokerage is based in Plano and has operated in Texas real estate since 2010.
