Common questions about Garland

What school district serves Garland, TX?

Garland is served by Garland ISD. Attendance boundaries can differ from city limits, so confirm the assigned campus for a specific address before making an offer.

What county is Garland, TX in?

Garland is located in Dallas County, in the Dallas-Fort Worth area of Texas.

Does Seeto Realty work with buyers and sellers in Garland?
