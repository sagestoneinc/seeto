Who we are

Seeto Realty is a small boutique real estate company working from a single office in Plano, handling every aspect of real estate transactions in the State of Texas — general residential, and the distressed and investment work most brokerages hand off.

Owner-operated means you deal with the person you hired. It also means we say no. If a property does not work, if a price is not supportable by the closed comparables, or if a distressed purchase carries risk you are not set up to absorb, you will hear that directly.

Common questions

Where is Seeto Realty located?

Seeto Realty works from one office at 700 W Spring Creek Pkwy #212, Plano, TX 75023, reachable at 972-509-7100. The firm serves both the Dallas-Fort Worth and Greater Houston markets.

Who founded Seeto Realty?

Seeto Realty was founded by Michael Seeto in 2010. He has held an active Texas Real Estate Commission broker licence (#496025-B) since 2002, with no disciplinary actions on record.

Does Seeto Realty handle distressed property?

Yes. HUD, REO and foreclosure work is handled in-house rather than referred out, and the Houston division led by David Seeto specialises in undervalued and distressed acquisition.
