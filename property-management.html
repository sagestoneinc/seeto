What management covers

Tenant screening

Income verification, rental history, and background review against consistent written criteria applied to every applicant.

Leasing and renewal

Marketing the unit, showing it, preparing the lease, and handling renewals before a vacancy opens up.

Rent collection

Consistent collection and clear escalation when payments are late, documented properly in case it ever matters.

Maintenance coordination

Vendor dispatch for repairs, with a spending threshold you set for what needs your approval first.

Inspections

Move-in and move-out condition documentation, which is what makes a deposit dispute resolvable.

Owner reporting

Statements showing income, expenses, and what happened at the property, so your accountant is not reconstructing the year in April.

Management questions

What does property management cost?

Management fees are typically a percentage of collected rent, with a separate leasing fee when a new tenant is placed. We quote both in writing for your specific property before you commit.

Who handles repairs and emergencies?

We coordinate vendors and handle emergency calls. You set a dollar threshold above which we contact you for approval before proceeding, so routine work does not stall and larger spending stays your decision.

What happens if a tenant stops paying?

We follow the notice and escalation process Texas law requires, keep you informed at each stage, and coordinate counsel if the matter proceeds to eviction. Consistent documentation from the start is what makes that process work.
