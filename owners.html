Owner questions

What does management cost?

Management fees are typically a percentage of collected rent, with a separate leasing fee when a new tenant is placed. Both are quoted in writing for your specific property before you commit — we do not publish a single rate because unit type, condition and location genuinely change the work involved.

Who approves repairs?

You set a dollar threshold. Work below it proceeds so routine issues do not stall; anything above it comes to you for approval first.

What happens if a tenant stops paying?

We follow the notice and escalation process Texas law requires, keep you informed at each stage, and coordinate counsel if it proceeds to eviction. Consistent documentation from move-in is what makes that process work.

Do you manage properties outside Dallas-Fort Worth?

Yes. Seeto Realty works across both the Dallas-Fort Worth and Greater Houston markets, with agents covering the Houston side.
