How the process runs

1. Get your financing straight

Talk to a lender before touring. A pre-approval sets your real budget and makes your offer credible when inventory moves quickly.

2. Narrow the map

We work out which cities and school attendance zones actually fit your budget, so you spend weekends on homes you could genuinely buy.

3. Tour and compare

We walk homes with you and point out what a photo set will not show: drainage, foundation movement, road noise, and deferred maintenance.

4. Offer with evidence

Offers are built on what comparable homes actually closed at, not on list prices, plus the option and earnest money terms that fit the situation.

5. Option period diligence

Texas gives buyers a negotiated option period. We use it for inspection, repair negotiation, and a decision you can still walk away from.

6. Close

We coordinate the title company, lender, and survey so the closing date holds and you know your final numbers before you sign.

Buying questions

How much do I need for a down payment in Texas?

It depends on the loan. Conventional loans commonly start around 3 to 5 percent, FHA at 3.5 percent, and VA loans can require nothing down for eligible buyers. Ask a lender to price several options against your situation before assuming you need 20 percent.

What is an option period?

A Texas option period is a negotiated window, typically a few days to about a week, during which the buyer may terminate for any reason in exchange for a non-refundable option fee. It is when inspections happen and repairs are negotiated.

Does the buyer pay the agent?

Compensation is negotiable and is agreed in writing before you tour homes. We explain exactly what you would owe and what, if anything, a seller is offering, before you sign anything.

How long does buying take?

Once an offer is accepted, a financed purchase in Texas commonly closes in about 30 to 45 days. Cash purchases can close faster. Finding the right home is usually the longer part of the process.
