How we work

Boutique means you deal with the person you hired. There is no handoff to a junior associate after the first meeting, and no call centre between you and an answer.

It also means we say no. If a property does not work, if a price is not supportable by the comparable sales, or if a distressed purchase carries risk you are not set up to absorb, you will hear that directly. A transaction that should not happen is worse for you than no transaction.
