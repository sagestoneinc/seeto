Who we are

Seeto Realty is a small boutique real estate company with offices in Plano and Houston, handling every aspect of real estate transactions in the State of Texas — general residential, and the distressed and investment work most brokerages hand off.

Owner-operated means you deal with the person you hired. It also means we say no. If a property does not work, if a price is not supportable by the closed comparables, or if a distressed purchase carries risk you are not set up to absorb, you will hear that directly.
