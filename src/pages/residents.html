Resident questions

How do I pay rent?

Through the resident portal at seetorealty.appfolio.com. You can make a one-off payment or set up a recurring one. If you have not activated your portal account, call the office at 972-509-7100 and we will send the invitation again.

How do I report a maintenance problem?

Submit it through the portal so it is logged and trackable, and attach photos where they help. For an emergency — active water, no heat, anything affecting safety — call the office directly at 972-509-7100 rather than waiting on a portal request.

What do I need to apply for a rental?

Applications are submitted online and reviewed against written criteria applied to every applicant: income verification, rental history and a background check. Specific requirements are listed with each available unit.

Who do I contact about my lease?

The office at 972-509-7100, or info@seetorealty.com. Lease documents are also available in your portal.

Need something we haven't covered?

Call the office and you will reach the firm, not a queue.
